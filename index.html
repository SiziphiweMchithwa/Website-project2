About Us

Welcome to Algoa Bus

Algoa Bus Company, headquartered in Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), South Africa, has been a significant part of public transport in the Eastern Cape since its founding in 1879. Serving Nelson Mandela Bay and nearby towns like Uitenhage and Despatch, Algoa Bus offers a reliable network of routes connecting central city areas, residential suburbs, and major community destinations such as the beachfront, shopping complexes, and universities. With services designed to accommodate daily commuters and regional travelers alike, the company emphasizes accessibility and efficiency, including features like a contactless smart card system for convenient travel.
